## 1. The site

**HostScope** is an independent, English-language research site about Hostinger.
It earns money through the Hostinger affiliate programme. Editorial stance:
useful first, promotional second. We are pro-Hostinger but we publish the
downsides — that is what makes the recommendations believable and what makes
the affiliate links convert.

**Reader:** someone who is deciding whether to buy Hostinger, which plan to
buy, or who already bought it and is now stuck. Often a beginner. Frequently
price-sensitive. Sometimes a developer evaluating VPS.

**Tone:** plain, specific, confident. Short sentences. Concrete numbers.
Second person ("you"). No hype words like "amazing", "game-changing",
"revolutionary". No emoji. No exclamation marks in body copy. British or
American spelling is fine as long as it is consistent inside a single file —
use American spelling.

**Never do this:**
- Never invent a statistic, a test result, or a customer quote.
- Never claim we ran a test we did not describe concretely.
- Never state a price without checking it against the fact sheet below.
- Never say "Hostinger is the best host" without qualifying it against
  a specific use case.
- Never put an affiliate link in the markdown body. Write normal internal
  links only (e.g. `[Hostinger's pricing page](/pricing/)`). The site injects
  affiliate CTAs automatically.

## 3. Verified facts — product and company

- Hostinger was founded in **2004** and is headquartered in **Kaunas,
  Lithuania**. It is one of the largest web hosts in the world.
- Hostinger says it serves **5 million+ customers across 178 countries**.
- Published uptime commitment: **99.9%**. Every plan carries a
  **30-day money-back guarantee**.
- **hPanel** is Hostinger's own control panel. It is not cPanel. It is
  generally considered easier for beginners: a left sidebar with Dashboard,
  Websites, Domains, Emails, VPS, Marketing, Files, Databases, Advanced,
  Account.
- **LiteSpeed** web server with the LSCache plugin for WordPress, plus
  built-in CDN and Cloudflare-protected nameservers.
- **Free automatic website migration** — you submit a request and Hostinger's
  team moves the site for you, usually in about 20 minutes, with no downtime.
- **Free SSL** certificates, installed automatically, unlimited on most plans.
- **Free WHOIS privacy** (valued at $9.99/yr) with domains.
- **Hostinger Agent** — an AI agent that can build a WordPress site, set up
  hosting/domains/DNS and troubleshoot common issues from a chat interface.
- **Hostinger Reach** — built-in email marketing.
- **Hostinger Connector** — lets AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor) manage
  your hosting through prompts.
- Support is 24/7 in **10+ languages** including English, Spanish, French,
  Portuguese, Chinese and Arabic. Median first response on live chat for
  English requests is around one minute.
- Data center regions include North America (Arizona, Massachusetts, New York),
  South America (Brazil), Europe (Lithuania, Netherlands, France, UK and
  others), South Africa, Asia (India, Indonesia, Singapore) and Australia.
- **Email sending limits (important):** PHP `mail()` and similar server-based
  sending is capped at **10 emails per minute and 100 per day**, resetting on a
  rolling 24-hour basis. Hostinger recommends switching to SMTP for reliable
  delivery. This is a real constraint people hit when running a WooCommerce
  store or a contact-form-heavy site.
- **All plans are paid upfront.** The advertised monthly rate is the total
  plan price divided by the number of months. This is the single most common
  misunderstanding among new buyers.
- Hostinger's WordPress hosting is recommended by WordPress.org.
- The **Web Business** plan was retired and is now sold as **Unlimited** on
  most pages. Existing Business customers keep their old limits.

## 4. The frontmatter contract — follow it exactly

Every article is a Markdown file in `src/content/articles/`. The frontmatter is
validated by a Zod schema; if you add a field that is not in the list below, or
miss a required one, **the build fails**.

```markdown
---
title: 'Sentence case headline, under 65 characters'
description: 'Meta description, 120-160 characters, includes the primary keyword and a reason to click.'
pubDate: 2026-05-12
updatedDate: 2026-09-10
author: 'maya-ross'
category: 'review'
tags: ['hostinger review', 'shared hosting', 'wordpress']
primaryKeyword: 'hostinger review'
affiliateNotice: true
featured: false
readingTime: 11
rating: 4.5
relatedPlans: ['unlimited', 'cloud-startup']
relatedArticles: ['is-hostinger-good', 'cheapest-way-to-buy-hostinger']
faqs:
  - question: 'A question a real buyer would type into Google'
    answer: 'A direct 2-4 sentence answer. No marketing language.'
---

Body markdown starts here.
```

**Field rules**

- `author` — one of `maya-ross`, `daniel-okafor`, `priya-nair`.
  - `maya-ross` → reviews, buying guides, deals
  - `daniel-okafor` → VPS, performance, troubleshooting
  - `priya-nair` → WordPress, WooCommerce, email, tutorials
- `category` — exactly one of `review`, `tutorial`, `guide`, `deals`,
  `comparison`, `troubleshooting`.
- `pubDate` / `updatedDate` — `YYYY-MM-DD`, no quotes, no time component.
  `pubDate` between 2025-01-15 and 2026-08-30. `updatedDate` >= `pubDate` and
  <= 2026-09-17.
- `rating` — **only** on `category: review` files. Omit otherwise.
- `steps` — optional. Include only for genuine step-by-step tutorials, using
  `- name: '...'` / `text: '...'` pairs. It generates HowTo structured data.
- `faqs` — 4 to 7 entries on every article. These become FAQPage structured
  data, so they must be real questions with genuinely useful answers.
- `relatedPlans` — slugs from this list only: `premium`, `unlimited`,
  `cloud-startup`, `cloud-professional`, `cloud-enterprise`, `kvm-1`, `kvm-2`,
  `kvm-4`, `kvm-8`, `hostinger-ai-builder`.
- `relatedArticles` — slugs of other articles in this batch or the wider set.
  Only reference slugs that actually exist; the build validates them loosely,
  but broken links are a quality failure.
- Omit `heroImage` entirely — the site generates its own artwork.

## 5. Body requirements

- **Length:** 1,100–2,000 words. Long enough to be genuinely useful, short
  enough that nobody skims past the CTA.
- **Structure:** `##` sections with descriptive headings. Use `###` for
  sub-points. Never skip from `##` to `####`.
- **Open with the answer.** The first two paragraphs must tell the reader what
  they came for. No "In today's digital landscape…" throat-clearing.
- **Include at least one table** in guides, comparisons and pricing articles.
  Markdown tables only, no HTML.
- **Include at least one blockquote** (`>`) with a practical rule of thumb.
- Use `-` for unordered lists and `1.` for ordered lists.
- Include real, specific detail: inode counts, PHP worker limits, renewal
  percentages, the 10-emails-per-minute cap, the 48-month upfront payment
  reality, the difference between SSD on Premium and NVMe above it.
- **Internal links:** 3–6 per article, using root-relative paths such as
  `/pricing/`, `/plans/unlimited/`, `/coupons/`, `/tools/plan-finder/`,
  `/blog/some-slug/`, `/compare/hostinger-vs-bluehost/`. Descriptive anchor
  text, never "click here".
- **Do not** add a call-to-action button or any affiliate URL. The layout adds
  those.
- **Do not** add an FAQ section at the bottom of the body — put FAQs in
  frontmatter; the layout renders them.
