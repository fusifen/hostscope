## What you are actually buying

Hostinger checkout is short — about four screens — but two decisions on it matter more than everything else: which term you pick, and which add-ons you decline. Get those right and the rest is a five-minute form.

The one thing to understand before you start: **every Hostinger plan is paid upfront.** The "$2.99/mo" headline is the total plan price divided by the number of months in the term. On Premium's 48-month term that is $143.52 charged once, not $2.99 a month.

Everything below assumes you have already decided roughly which plan you want. If you have not, work through our [plan decision guide](/blog/which-hostinger-plan-should-you-choose/) first — the plan choice matters far more than the checkout mechanics.

## The purchase flow, step by step

### Step 1: Choose a plan

You will land on a plan comparison page. Three web hosting tiers matter for most buyers:

| Plan | Websites | CPU / RAM | Storage | Backups | 48-mo price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premium | 3 | 1 core / 2 GB | 20 GB SSD | Weekly | $2.99/mo |
| Unlimited | Unlimited | 2 cores / 3 GB | 50 GB NVMe | Daily | $3.99/mo |
| Cloud Startup | Unlimited (cap 100) | 4 cores / 4 GB | 100 GB NVMe | Daily + on-demand | $7.99/mo |

If you are unsure, Unlimited is the safer default. The extra dollar a month buys daily backups, a dedicated IP, a second CPU core and NVMe storage — four separate problems solved at once.

### Step 2: Choose your term

This is the screen where the price changes most. Hostinger offers a long discount term (48 months), shorter terms, and a monthly option. The longer the term, the lower the monthly rate and the larger the payment today.

| Term | Premium effective rate | Premium total upfront |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 48 months | $2.99/mo | $143.52 |
| 24 months | Higher than the 48-month rate | Read the exact figure at checkout |
| 12 months | Roughly $6.99/mo | Roughly $83.88 |
| Monthly | Highest rate available | Billed monthly, no term lock |

The 48-month and 12-month figures were checked on 2026-09-17. Hostinger generates the 24-month and monthly rates at checkout and they move with promotions, so read them off the screen rather than trusting a number from an article — including this one.

> **Rule of thumb:** pick the longest term you are confident about, not the longest term on offer. A four-year commitment to a project you might abandon in eight months is not a saving, it is a bet.

### Step 3: The domain step

You will be asked for a domain name. Two paths:

- **Register a new one.** Free for the first 12 months with every web hosting plan. After that it renews at the standard rate, typically $9.99–$15.99/yr for a .com. Free WHOIS privacy (valued at $9.99/yr) is included.
- **Use one you already own.** Either point its nameservers at Hostinger or transfer the registration. Transferring extends the registration by a year in most cases and requires an EPP or authorisation code from your current registrar.

If you are migrating a site rather than starting fresh, you can also take the free automatic migration: submit a request and Hostinger's team moves the site, usually in about 20 minutes with no downtime.

### Step 4: The add-on screen — what to decline

This is where the order total grows. Hostinger's upsells are not deceptive, but most of them are unnecessary at the entry tier.

| Add-on | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Priority support | Decline | You already get 24/7 priority support on every plan |
| Extra backup storage | Decline | Premium's weekly backups are the real limit, not the storage size |
| SEO tools | Decline | Free WordPress plugins cover the same ground |
| Extra storage | Usually decline | 20 GB on Premium is fine for a text and image site; upgrade the plan instead if you need more |
| Domain privacy | Accept if offered as paid | It is already free with Hostinger domains — do not pay twice |
| Website builder bundle | Depends | Only useful if you plan to use the builder instead of WordPress |

The rule is simple: if the add-on is solving a problem you do not have yet, decline it. You can buy almost all of these later, and buying them later costs about the same.

### Step 5: Apply a promo code

There is a promo or coupon field on the order summary, usually behind a small expandable link. Enter the code and apply it before you pay — it does not work retroactively.

Two things to know: codes are often restricted to specific terms or plans, and a code that worked last month may have expired. We keep a current list on the [Hostinger coupons page](/coupons/). Check it immediately before checkout, not a week earlier.

### Step 6: Payment

Hostinger accepts major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and in some regions local payment methods and bank transfer. The full term amount is charged once. If your card has a low limit, the 48-month total on Cloud Startup ($383.52) may not go through — worth checking before you commit to the term.

### Step 7: Immediately after checkout

- Check your email for the account confirmation and set a password.
- **Turn on two-factor authentication.** Your hosting account controls your domain and your site. This is not optional in practice.
- Your hosting is live immediately; the domain takes time to propagate if it was registered elsewhere.

### Step 8: Get the site online

From hPanel:

1. Use the one-click installer to deploy WordPress or another application. It takes about a minute.
2. Confirm the free SSL certificate has been issued and is active. It is automatic, but check.
3. Install a caching plugin. On WordPress that means LSCache — it is the single biggest performance lever you control.
4. If your domain is registered elsewhere, update the nameservers to Hostinger's and allow up to 24 hours for propagation.

### Step 9: Diary the renewal

Set a reminder for roughly two months before your term ends. Premium renews at $10.99/mo and Unlimited at $16.99/mo — both well above the intro rate. That reminder is when you decide whether to renew, upgrade or move. Our [renewal price guide](/blog/hostinger-renewal-prices-explained/) walks through the maths.

## Common mistakes at checkout

- **Assuming it is a monthly bill.** It is not. Check the order total, not the per-month figure.
- **Buying the cheapest plan for a store.** Premium's weekly backups and single core are the wrong foundation for WooCommerce.
- **Buying the longest term for an untested idea.** The 30-day money-back guarantee covers the hosting fee, but you cannot get a refund on a registered domain.
- **Ignoring the domain renewal.** It is free for one year, then it is a normal annual cost.
- **Skipping the promo box.** It is easy to miss and it is a real discount.
- **Not checking the data center region.** Pick the region nearest your audience at signup. Changing it later means migrating.
