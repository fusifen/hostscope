## The short version

Hostinger gives you **30 days** to change your mind on any plan. If you request
a refund inside that window, the hosting fee comes back. The guarantee is
genuine and it applies to every plan, from Premium at $2.99/mo up to the VPS and
cloud tiers.

The complications are all in the edges. Domain registration fees are typically
not refundable. Some add-on services sit outside the guarantee. And the 30 days
start at purchase, not at the moment you first log in — so if you buy a plan and
leave it sitting for five weeks before building the site, you have already used
the window.

This guide covers what is covered, what is not, how to ask, and how long the
money takes.

## What the 30-day window covers

The guarantee covers the **hosting fee for the plan you bought**. That includes:

- Shared web hosting plans — Premium, Unlimited and Cloud Startup
- Cloud hosting tiers above Cloud Startup
- VPS plans, including KVM 1 through KVM 8
- The AI website builder plans

The window runs for 30 days from the purchase date. It is a single window per
purchase, not per year and not renewable.

> Practical rule: treat day 25 as your decision deadline, not day 30. If you are
> going to request a refund, do it with five days of slack. Support queues and
> time zones do not care that you were technically inside the window.

## What is not covered

This is the part most refund guides skip.

| Item | Refundable? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting plan fee | Yes, within 30 days | The guarantee applies to the plan itself |
| Domain registration fee | Typically no | Registration is paid onward to the registry and cannot be reversed |
| Free domain claimed with a plan | Deducted from the refund | The domain has already been registered in your name |
| Some add-on services | Usually no | Third-party add-ons are provisioned and billed immediately |
| Renewal payments | Outside the guarantee | The 30-day window applies to the initial purchase |

The domain point matters most. Hostinger includes a free domain for the first
year on its web hosting plans. That domain is not literally free to Hostinger —
it pays a registry fee. If you take the domain and then refund the plan within
30 days, expect the standard domain registration fee to be subtracted from the
amount you get back.

If you plan to refund, the cleanest sequence is to request the refund and deal
with the domain separately. You can keep the domain and pay for it, or let it
lapse. Either way, ask support to confirm the deduction before you agree to it.

## How to request a refund

There are two routes, and both work.

### Route 1: through hPanel

1. Log in to hPanel.
2. Open the **Account** area from the left sidebar.
3. Find your purchases or billing section.
4. Locate the plan you want to refund.
5. Look for the cancellation option and follow the prompts.

The exact labels move around as hPanel is updated, so treat the above as the
general path rather than a fixed menu. The two things you are looking for are
your purchase list and a cancel action.

### Route 2: through support

This is the route we recommend, because it creates a written record.

1. Open a support ticket from within hPanel, or start a live chat and ask to be
   escalated to a ticket.
2. State clearly: the plan name, the purchase date, and that you are requesting
   a refund under the 30-day money-back guarantee.
3. Ask for confirmation of the amount and any deductions.
4. Keep the ticket number.
5. Do not close the ticket until the refund appears.

Live chat first response on English requests is typically around a minute, which
makes chat a reasonable place to start — but ask for the ticket, because chat
transcripts are harder to reference later.

> Write the request, do not just describe it. A message that says "I would like
> to cancel and request a refund under the 30-day money-back guarantee for the
> Unlimited plan purchased on 3 August" is resolved faster than "I want to stop
> using this."

## Cancel, do not just stop using it

This is the single most common mistake. People stop logging in, assume the plan
has lapsed, and then find a renewal charge months later.

Stopping use is not cancelling. The plan stays active, the term runs out, and it
renews at the standard renewal rate — **$10.99/mo for Premium, $16.99/mo for
Unlimited, $25.99/mo for Cloud Startup**.

Two separate actions are needed:

- **Cancel the subscription** so it does not renew.
- **Request the refund** so the money comes back.

Do both, and confirm both in writing.

## How long the money takes

The refund is issued by Hostinger promptly after approval. The time it takes to
appear depends entirely on the payment method.

| Payment method | Typical time to appear |
| --- | --- |
| Credit or debit card | Several business days after the refund is issued |
| PayPal or wallet | Often faster than card, sometimes within a day |
| Bank transfer | Slowest — can take longer than card |
| Cryptocurrency or similar | Varies; usually returned in the same currency |

Card refunds are the slowest to appear because the delay is on the card
network's side, not Hostinger's. If a refund has been confirmed in a ticket and
has not appeared after a week, the next step is to ask your bank whether a
pending credit is visible, then go back to the ticket with that information.

## Practical tips

**Request in writing.** A ticket is evidence. A chat message is weaker.

**Keep the ticket number.** Every follow-up should reference it.

**Screenshot the purchase.** Your order confirmation shows the date, which is
what the 30-day window is measured from.

**Ask for the amount before you agree.** Confirm any domain deduction in
writing.

**Take a backup before you cancel.** Cancelling a plan will eventually remove the
files. Export anything you want to keep — database dump and file archive — before
the cancellation completes. If the plan is being refunded because the site is
moving elsewhere, do the migration first and the refund second.

**Do not buy a long term you are unsure about just because it is cheap.** The
48-month term on Premium is $143.52 upfront. The 30-day guarantee makes that a
low-risk experiment, but only if you actually run the experiment inside the
window. If you are genuinely undecided, a shorter term costs more per month and
carries less exposure. Our [price reference](/blog/hostinger-price-history/)
breaks down the difference in totals.

## A refund checklist

1. Confirm your purchase date and count the days.
2. Take a full backup of files and databases.
3. Open a ticket and state the plan, purchase date and the guarantee.
4. Ask for the expected refund amount and any deductions.
5. Cancel the subscription separately and confirm it.
6. Save the ticket number and screenshot the confirmation.
7. Watch your payment method for the credit, and follow up in the same ticket if
   it does not appear.
